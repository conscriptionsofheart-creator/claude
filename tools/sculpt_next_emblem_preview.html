Your emblem, in clay

Built by the next sculpt engine from your picture: each body is one rectangular rod, painted in a gradient and twisted, so its other face shows where it turns. The heads are spheres. It turns slowly. In the headset it appears beside you with Sculpt > Clay & shapes > Make the emblem.

Shaping the clay…

Drag the emblem to turn it yourself. Spin takes about ten seconds a turn and eases in and out; it never flashes.

Parts (each a clay layer)

Rods show two bars: the front face, then the back face that appears where the rod twists.
