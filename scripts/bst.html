Add a bone

Turn on the Bones view and click a bone dot: the new bone grows from it.
Skin radius 0 adds the bone and leaves the skin alone, so you can paint it by hand. A radius gives the skin inside it to the new bone (a bone sits a few centimetres inside the body, so the radius has to reach the surface: 8 to 15 cm for a limb), full at the bone and fading to nothing at the rim. For a tail, a hat or a cloak: put it by the bone, select it with the figure and use Duplicate as one object; it ties to the nearest bone.
