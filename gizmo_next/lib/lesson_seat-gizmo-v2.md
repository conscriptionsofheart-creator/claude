# Lesson draft: Walk to a chair and sit, with a pose from your library

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. A seat takes you only when you press its Sit tile.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Open the wrist menu. Press Objects, then Gizmos, then Seat. Put the seat down and turn it so its arrow points the way your knees should.
2. Turn Walk on. Walk up to the seat and look at it.
3. A Sit tile floats above it when you are close. Back away and it goes. Turn your back and it goes.
4. Face the seat and press the Sit tile with the ray. The edges fade, and you sit.
5. Look in the mirror. Your body sits properly, at its full size. Your head is where your eyes would be sitting down.
6. Push the stick forward and to the sides. Nothing happens. That is on purpose.
7. Pull the stick back. You stand up in front of the seat.
8. Tap the seat. Press Pose until it names a sit from your pose library. Sit again and watch it blend in over a moment.
9. Press Use Custom Exit Direction, then Exit Direction until it says Right. Sit and stand. You stand to the right of the seat.
10. Press Nudge up 2 cm and sit. You sit a little higher. Press Reset the nudge.
11. Press Who can use it until it says Guests. The tile no longer shows for you.

## What to try

- Put a seat on a sofa and turn Height from the surface on.
- Add a seat beat to a figure on the Beat strip and watch it sit in time.
- Try to sit where a friend or a figure is already sitting. It says the seat is taken.
