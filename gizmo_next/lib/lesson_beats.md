# Lesson draft: Make a figure wave with the Beat strip

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. Nothing plays until you press Play.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Put a figure in the scene and select it.
2. Pose it with its right hand up, above its head.
3. Open the wrist menu. Press Capture face, then Animate, then Beat strip.
4. Press Add beat. A card appears on the strip, marked 0.0 s. The clock moves on to 1.0 s.
5. Move the hand out to the right. Press Add beat. A second card appears at 1.0 s.
6. Move the hand out to the left. Press Add beat. A third card appears at 2.0 s.
7. Press Add beat again without moving anything. It says nothing changed, and no card is added. That is on purpose.
8. Press Play. The hand goes up, to the right, and to the left, and stops.
9. Tap the second card. The clock jumps to 1.0 s and the hand is out to the right. Tap the first card, then the third.
10. Press Stop. The figure goes back to how it was when you pressed Play.
11. Press Loop mode until it says Loop, then press Play. The wave goes round and round. Press Pause.
12. Press Loop mode until it says Yoyo. Press Play. It goes forward, then back. Press Stop.
13. Tap the second card, then press Bracket start here. Tap the third card, then press Bracket end here. Press Play. Only that part loops. Press Clear the bracket.
14. Tap the second card and press Delete this beat. The card goes. Press Undo and it comes back.

## What to try

- Press Gap between beats to make the gap half a second, then add beats.
- Press Add beat with look after changing a colour, and watch it blend, never flash.
- Play a song and press Play: the beats follow the song's time.
- Ask a friend to join. They watch what you play, and they cannot change it.

## Last step

Leave a note on what works and what does not. Say what you tried, where you were, and what you expected to happen.
