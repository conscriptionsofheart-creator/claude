# Lesson draft: Greet a friend at the door

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. You decide, your friend follows.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Place a Trigger box at the door. Record a short greeting on the Beat strip and name the range Greeting.
2. Tap the trigger. Add the start Play beats, Greeting. Press Armed.
3. Open the wrist menu. Press Settings, then Gizmos live, to turn it on.
4. Invite a friend through the guest door.
5. After a few seconds your friend sees your scene. They have no Save, Export or Download.
6. Your friend walks into the door box. The greeting plays for them, in step with you.
7. Press Stop on the Beat strip. It stops for them too.
8. Turn Gizmos live off. It goes off for them too.

## What to try
Put an Effects gizmo by the door with Confetti, and have the trigger start it. Sit on a seat and ask your friend whether it shows as taken.
