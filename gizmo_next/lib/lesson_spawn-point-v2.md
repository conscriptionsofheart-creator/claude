# Lesson draft: Add a second spawn point and set the order

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. Nobody is moved until Gizmos live is on, or you press Test arrive.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Open the wrist menu. Press Objects, then Gizmos, then Spawn point. A second spawn point comes out. The first one is still there.
2. Put it down somewhere else. Turn it so its arrow points the way a friend should face when they arrive.
3. Tap the second point. Its panel opens. The top row says the defaults: Gravity 9.81, Speed 4, Order 1, fade 0.4 seconds.
4. Press Order until it says 2. The first point stays at 1, so a friend lands on the first.
5. Press Pick until it says Take turns on the first point. Now the first friend goes to point 1, the next to point 2, then round again.
6. Press Check this point. It says the point is on the floor and in the open. Move it over a gap and press again: it says there is no floor, and it will never be used.
7. Stand a long way off and press Test arrive. You land on the point, facing its arrow, with a short fade. Nobody else moves.
8. Press Set Position Only until it says on, then Test arrive again. You land but keep the way you were facing. Set it back to off.
9. Open Settings and press Gizmos live. It says Gizmos live: on.
10. Ask a friend to join. They land on the first point. A second friend lands on the second.
11. On the second point press Who until it says Named person, and add your friend's name. Now only they can land there.

## What to try

- Press Respawn if I fall below, set it to minus 5 metres, and walk off a ledge. You are sent back, once.
- Press Snap spot on a point, then aim the walk teleport near it. The aim snaps to the point.
- Make a trigger that sends someone to a spawn point when they step in.
