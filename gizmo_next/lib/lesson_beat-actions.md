# Lesson draft: Say hello and change the shader on the Beat strip

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. Nothing plays until you press Play.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Make a short wave with the Beat strip: two pose beats, four seconds apart. Put the clock at half a second.
2. Open Narration and record a line for the figure: "hello friend". Keep it short.
3. Go back to Capture face, then Animate, then Beat strip. Find the row Add voice: hello friend, with its length.
4. Press it. A card appears at half a second with a voice icon. The clock moves on to the end of the recording.
5. Select the card and look at its hold. It is the length of your recording. Press Hold longer if you want a pause after it, then Hold shorter to undo that.
6. Press Play. The figure starts to wave and, at half a second, says hello. Its mouth moves while the voice plays.
7. Press Stop in the middle of the line. The voice stops at once and the figure goes back.
8. Change the figure's shader. Put the clock at two seconds and press Add shader beat. A card appears with a look icon.
9. Press Play. The shader changes smoothly as the clock reaches it. The clothes do not change, because the beat only carries the shader.
10. Add a second shader beat a little after the first. It moves a bit later, and the toast says so. That keeps looks from flashing.
11. Try Add clothes beat with something that is not in your cleared wardrobe. It tells you the piece was left out.

## What to try

- Add a voice beat, then another recording after it. The second waits for the first to finish.
- Press Pause during a line. It stops the voice. Press Play and it carries on with the next beats.
- Ask a friend to join. They see the poses and looks move. Your voice stays on your side.
