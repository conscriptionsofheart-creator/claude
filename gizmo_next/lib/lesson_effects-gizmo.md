# Lesson draft: Make confetti, sparks and a trail

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. Nothing plays until Gizmos live is on.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Open the wrist menu. Press Objects, then Gizmos, then Effects. Put it down where you want the effect.
2. Turn Gizmos live on.
3. Tap the Effects gizmo. Press Preset until it says Confetti.
4. Press Play once. A burst of confetti, then it ends by itself.
5. Press Loop. It keeps going.
6. Press Start colour a few times. The colour glides to the new one. It never flashes.
7. Press Stop. Everything clears at once.
8. Press Tab to switch to Trail. Press Follow until it says Hand right. Press Loop and wave your hand. A ribbon follows it.
9. Press Stop. The ribbon is gone.

## What to try
Make a beat of kind Effect so the confetti starts at the right moment in a scene. Turn Hide gizmos on and watch the effect keep playing.
