# Lesson draft: Change how you walk, jump and fly

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. Check that Settings does not already have Speed and Gravity rows first.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Open the wrist menu. Press Settings, then Walk settings.
2. Press Player Speed until it says 8. Turn Walk on and walk. You move faster, and the edges of your view darken a little.
3. Press Player Speed until it says 4. The darkening goes.
4. Press Player Gravity until it says 0. Jump. You float.
5. Press Player Gravity until it says 9.81. You fall like Earth.
6. Turn Fly on. Push the second stick up and you rise. Fly through a wall. You pass through it, but never through the floor.
7. Turn Fly off in the air. You drop to the floor under you.
8. Sit on a seat and push the stick. Nothing moves.

## What to try
Press Fly speed cap and fly across the room at different speeds.
