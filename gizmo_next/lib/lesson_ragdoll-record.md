# Lesson draft: Record a fall and play it again

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. Nothing records until you press Record fall.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Put a figure in the scene and select it.
2. Turn Gravity on, as in the rag doll lesson, so the figure can fall. Keep it standing for now.
3. Open the wrist menu. Press Capture face, then Animate, then Beat strip.
4. Press Record fall while the figure is still held. It says to turn Gravity on and let go first. That is on purpose.
5. Let the figure go so it starts to fall, and press Record fall at the same moment. Watch it land.
6. Press Stop recording the fall. It tells you how many seconds it kept.
7. Stand the figure up and move it to a different spot. Turn it round.
8. Press Play fall. The figure falls the same way, from where it is now. Nothing is being simulated.
9. Press Stop the fall. The figure goes back where it was.
10. Press Add ragdoll beat: Fall 1. A card appears on the strip.
11. Add a pose beat a few seconds later and press Play. The figure falls at the time of the card, once.
12. Press Undo last recording. The fall is gone, and so is its card's clip.

## What to try

- Record a second, different fall and put both on the strip.
- Set Loop mode to Loop and watch the fall play again on each pass.
- Ask a friend to join. They see the poses and looks. They cannot record.

## Last step

Leave a note on what works and what does not. Say what you tried, where you were, and what you expected to happen.
