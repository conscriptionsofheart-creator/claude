# Lesson draft: Place a gizmo and turn Gizmos live on

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. A gizmo does nothing until Gizmos live is on.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Put the headset on and open the wrist menu. Press Objects, then Gizmos.
2. Press a gizmo tile, for example Trigger box. A see-through box comes out and settles in your hand. Its picture and name are on every side.
3. Put it down where you want it. The box is quiet. Nothing happens yet.
4. Squeeze the trigger on the box to grab it. Move it, then let go.
5. Grab it with both hands, one near each side, and pull the hands apart or push them together. The box gets bigger or smaller.
6. Hover over one of its handles. It opens into move, scale and turn. Use Fine Tune for small steps.
7. Tap the box once, quickly. Its panel opens at the top of your wrist menu. Press Lock and try to grab it. It stays put. Press Unlock.
8. Press Duplicate. A second box appears next to it with its own name.
9. Press Delete on the copy. Open Objects, then Recently deleted, and press the copy to bring it back.
10. Press Back until you reach the wrist menu. Press Settings, then Gizmos live. The row now says Gizmos live: on. Armed gizmos run now.
11. Press Gizmos live again. It says Gizmos live: off. Everything stops.
12. Open Objects, then Gizmos, then Hide gizmos. The boxes disappear but anything that was running keeps running. Press Show gizmos to bring them back.
13. Take a Photo. No gizmo box is in the picture.
14. Save the scene, start a New scene, then open the saved one. The boxes are back, and nothing started by itself.

## What to try

- Place two gizmos and give them different names.
- Turn Gizmos live on, then off, and watch the short list under it: it says what ran.
- Hide gizmos with Gizmos live on, and check that things still happen.
- Ask a friend to join the room. They see what you have on, and they cannot change it.

## Last step

Leave a note on what works and what does not. Say what you tried, where you were, and what you expected to happen.
