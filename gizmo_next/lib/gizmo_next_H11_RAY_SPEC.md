# Ray spec H11: the real-ray test of the small gizmos

For the local seat, after the edits in `README_H11.md`. Atelier and BSA, headset emulator, real ray presses. Numbers to read are in brackets.

1. Add a Text sign. Set the words to a long sentence, Auto Fit on. [the letters shrink to fit; nothing spills; the letters are readable at 2 m]
2. Turn Auto Fit off and Fixed Font Size to 128. [the letters stay big; words that do not fit say so in the log]
3. Set Visible off. [the words disappear, the small box stays]. On. [back]
4. Turn Hide gizmos on. [the box goes, the words stay]
5. Paste 1500 characters. [1000 stay]
6. Add a Door and pick a scene. Press Go with Gizmos live off. [nothing opens; the message says to turn live on]. Turn live on and press Go. [the scene opens]. Open the scene with the door in it. [it does not open by itself]
7. A friend through the guest door: only doors to shared scenes show; Go to an unshared scene is refused. [plain message]
8. Pick Softwood and Super ice on an object and drop it. [Softwood slides less than Ice; Super ice slides more]
9. Add 21 lights. [the warning appears once]. Remove one. [no warning]
10. Add a mirror and raise Render Radius. [the cost number rises; scale stays equal on all three axes]
11. Make two camera beats 0.1 s apart. [they are spread to 0.4 s; each is a 0.4 s dip, never a flash; read the screen brightness: no change faster than 2.5 a second]
12. On a sound gizmo press Pitch up three times and Min distance. [the sound rises three semitones; the near volume is full out to the min distance]. Press Fade. [never under 0.4 s]
13. Save, New scene, open the saved one. [everything is back; nothing plays or opens]
