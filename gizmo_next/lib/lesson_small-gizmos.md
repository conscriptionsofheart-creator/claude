# Lesson draft: A sign and a door

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Open the wrist menu. Press Objects, then Gizmos, then Text sign. Put the sign above the door.
2. Tap the sign. Press Words until it says Welcome. The letters fit the box.
3. Press Auto Fit to turn it off, then Fixed Font Size. The letters stay the same size.
4. Press Visible to hide the words, and again to show them.
5. Press Objects, then Gizmos, then Door. Put it on the wall.
6. Tap the door. Press Scene until it names the scene you want.
7. Turn Gizmos live on. Tap the door and press Go. The scene opens.
8. Share the scene with a friend. They see the door. A scene you did not share does not show a door for them.

## What to try
Add a trigger box that plays your greeting, and a Text sign that says Welcome, by the same door.
