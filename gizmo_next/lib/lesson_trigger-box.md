# Lesson draft: Greet a friend at the door with a Trigger box

Draft only. Not voiced and not registered in the app. It waits until the menus settle.
Works the same in the Atelier and the BSA headset. A trigger does nothing until it is Armed and Gizmos live is on.

## Steps (the exact taps, in order)

1. Make a short greeting on the Beat strip: a figure waves, with a voice if you like. Press Save range: Greeting.
2. Open the wrist menu. Press Objects, then Gizmos, then Trigger box. A small box comes out and settles in your hand.
3. Put it in a doorway. Hover it: the whole volume lights up. Move away and it shrinks back to the small box.
4. Grab a handle and stretch the box until it fills the doorway. Or grab with both hands and pull apart.
5. Tap the box. Its panel opens. Set Players to Guests only.
6. Set When to Someone enters. Set How often to Once per visitor.
7. Press Add: Play beats Greeting. It appears in the list.
8. Press Test it. The greeting plays, and the log says it was a test.
9. Press Armed. It says Armed: on.
10. Open Settings and press Gizmos live. It says Gizmos live: on.
11. Walk through the box yourself. Nothing happens, because it is only for guests.
12. Ask a friend to join and walk in. The greeting plays. Ask them to walk out and in again. It does not play twice.
13. Press Hide gizmos. The box vanishes and still works.

## What to try

- Press the delay on a start to make it wait half a second.
- Add a second start after the greeting, such as a sound.
- Set When to Both to say goodbye as well.
- Make another trigger with Just me and try the template Greeting at the door.
