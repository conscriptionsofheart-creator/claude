Ruth (2026-10-07) uses a Meta Quest. She wants no cable, no typing, nothing but "put the headset on and click an icon". The door is `pose_editor/vr_link/vr_link.py` (https 8443, key in secret.txt, remembers her headset address). Her first VR scene is the Atelier icy-lake waltz (`?vrscene=lake`).

**Why:** the first run placed her under the floor, tiny, with the dance fast and flickering, and it made her sick.
**How to apply:** VR always starts paused and slower, plain floor not the mirror floor, no flashing, nothing moves her without her input; A/B/X/Y or a stick click freezes everything. Never put the app on the public internet (private and ERP content): LAN only with the key. Test with the IWER emulator, then ask her for one short real-headset check. See [[no-flashing-animations-seizure-safety]].
