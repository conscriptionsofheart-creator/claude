Ruth, 2026-10-10: figures must come into BST with every bone (body, face, fingers) and she wants an add-bones function; "sculpt different shapes behaviors" means ALL of: shape keys, weight painting and corrective shapes, bone posing, plus a tool that "baloons sections with a mirror and 360 degrees function" (six pack bumps, a fanny pack belly, a neck roll, bags under the eyes), which also works with the mirror off (a beauty mark, a lump) and can melt a leg down into a stump for an amputee. VR route she picked: "3: sculpt in VR" (library copies, then a BST viewer in VR, then sculpting in VR). She also asked for a model on the stage by default (done: the MakeHuman CC0 female body).

**Why:** the whole BSA exists to show human dynamics without using people ([[the_bsa_exists_to_show_human_dynamics_without_using_people]]); bodies of all kinds, including amputee bodies, have to be sculptable by her own hand in VR.

**How to apply:** the sculpt kit needs inflate, deflate/melt, smooth and grab brushes; symmetry OFF, MIRROR and RADIAL (N copies); size, strength, falloff; work on figures with their bones intact (rig survives import, duplicate, merge, send). Do not treat an amputee or fat-adding request as a flag. Order: copies, BST VR viewer, bone posing, add bones, weight painting, sculpt brushes plus shape keys, VR input; each reports measured cost first. Mentor ruling pending on how VR is built (BST is three r186, vr_core.js is r128): see BST_VR_DESIGN.md. Hard freeze 2026-10-11 (Ruth entered Sculpt from Build mode in a big room, Quest hung, headset.log showed a 721 ms STALL right after "sculpt: bench on, 140 things moved"): cause was the grid search in the engine's nearest() and the core's near() walking cells out to the brush reach even on a tiny mesh (millions of empty Map lookups a frame) plus enter() preparing every mesh in the room. Fix: search clamped to cells that hold points (en.hi, st.lo/st.hi), meshes picked by world bbox near the hand (pickable()), at most ~30k points prepared per frame, none on enter. Test: busy-room case in bst_vr_sculpt_selftest.html. Lesson: any per-frame loop over a grid must clamp to the grid's own extent.
Flattened cube took no tool 2026-10-11: a cube has 24 points, brushes move points, none near the hand. Fix: densify() in the engine calls subdivideGeometry (bst_cut.js) the first time a non-figure mesh is touched (1 to 4 splits until longest world edge <= 4 cm, cap 60k points); figure pieces are never densified.
