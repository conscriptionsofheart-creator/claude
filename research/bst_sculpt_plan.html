BST sculpt plan

10 October 2026. The sculpt plan for BST, the body sculpting tool (open BST).

You said the whole point of this is the VR PART and i sculpt with my hands. You asked for the sculpting tools that live in Adobe Substance and Gravity Sketch, like the cutting tool, and the opposite of inflate (indent). Indent is built. The cutting tool is not. The tables below list every tool I found in their own pages, whether BST has it, and how your hands could do it in the headset. The hand gestures are our proposal, because both programs are built for controllers.

Adobe Substance 3D Modeler

  • Controllers only. No page I read mentions hand tracking. That is silence, not a no.
  • Windows PC only. A Quest has to be linked to the PC. It does not run on a Mac.
  • Paid: $59.99 a month in the Substance 3D Collection, or a free beta that Adobe says is not for real work.
  • We learn from its design and use none of its code.

Gravity Sketch

  • The modelling tools are free, and making anything needs a headset.
  • The free plan exports only OBJ and USDZ. GLB and FBX are paid. Whether free exports may be sold is UNVERIFIED (terms not read).
  • Controllers. Hand tracking is not documented.
  • Prices were rolling out through 2026; check its pricing page again before relying on them.

Sources: SUBSTANCE_MODELER_RESEARCH, GRAVITY_SKETCH_RESEARCH and SKETCHFAB_RESEARCH, 10 October 2026. UNVERIFIED means the source did not say. Each tool name links to the page it came from.

Already in BST and the app

Built only, read from the code. Where it lives, headset first, then desktop.

  • Fingertip and pinch as the brush, with the sculpt bench that hides everything but the figureheadset: Pose face > More > Sculpt; desktop: none
  • Inflate, Indent, Melt, Smooth, and a Grab brushheadset: Sculpt menu; desktop: Sculpt tab has Inflate, Deflate (Indent), Melt and Smooth, no Grab button
  • Brush size and strengthheadset: Bigger, Smaller, Stronger, Softer; desktop: sliders
  • Symmetry: off, mirror, 360 round the nearest boneheadset: Symmetry button; desktop: Sculpt tab
  • Shape keys: every sculpt is a named 0 to 1 slider that saves in the .glbheadset: New shape key and Keys; desktop: Sculpt tab
  • One undo per strokeheadset: Undo stroke, Redo stroke, round arrows; desktop: Undo and Redo
  • Save the sculpted figure to the libraryheadset: Save figure; desktop: Send to library
  • Hand menu, Walk, grip to move the world, both hands to zoom, frame-rate tileheadset: Atelier menu; desktop: not needed
  • Snap: on or off, to objects and grid, grid size, angleheadset: Settings > Snap; desktop: none
  • Shapes (the Atelier's solids)headset: Pose face > Objects > Add a shape; desktop: BST Shapes tab
  • Copies in a row (3 or 5, along the red, green or blue way), Lock and Unlock, Keep my grip, Stick to my hand, Select several kept togetherheadset: Objects panel; desktop: none
  • Union, subtract and intersect for two Atelier shapes (lib/three_csg.js, three-csg-ts 3.1.5, MIT, licence in three_csg_LICENSE.txt). It splits triangles heavily, so the new cut work proposes three-bvh-csg. No skins, not for loaded filesheadset: Cut and join in the Atelier; desktop: none
  • Hair brush that paints low-poly hairheadset: Hair face (the other brush that already exists in VR); desktop: Atelier Hair tab
  • Bones: see them, pose any bone, Add bone with a skin radiusheadset: not in BST, the Atelier has its own Pose and Heal; desktop: BST Bones tab
  • Paint skin: bone weights with a Weights view (add, remove, smooth, size, strength)headset: not yet, the Atelier has its own Joint painter; desktop: BST Paint skin
  • On stage: select, Shift-click adds, move / turn / size handles, hide, rename, delete with Recently deleted, duplicate, Duplicate as one object (bones kept)headset: none; desktop: BST On stage tab
  • Triangle meter, Shaded / Wireframe / Clay views, delivery copies for PC, Quest and web (same detail, each flagged against its own limit)headset: none; desktop: BST

The tools

In the headset and On the desktop: Built means it works today. Partly means some of it works. Not yet means nothing is built. n/a means the tool only makes sense in the headset. The headset is the Atelier headset menu (the BST page itself has no VR button), and it comes first.

How cost is stated

  • Units are the weighted units your weekly meter uses. Clock is machine time, not human hours.
  • Measured: phase 0 was planned at about 3M and landed at 10.9M units, 3.6 times the plan. Only that phase has a recorded token total.
  • Measured clock, from commit times today: sculpt into shape keys with mirror and 360 took about 14 min after the brush commit, Add bone about 5 min, Paint skin about 22 min, the headset sculpt engine about 6 min. These are gaps between commits, so a rough clock. Their tokens were not recorded.
  • Anything marked "estimate" has no measured match. Its basis is in Notes.
  • Libraries: three-bvh-csg is MIT, manifold-3d is Apache 2.0, meshoptimizer is MIT. Re-check each at its source before it ships.

Order of work

The headset comes first, since the whole point is the VR part.

  1. Feel the hand sculpt that is built. Open the headset menu, Pose face > More > Sculpt, pinch a fingertip on the figure. Tell me what is wrong. I fix it before building more. estimate: 1 to 2M units for the fixes, 20 to 40 min. Basis: none measured; depends on what you find.
  2. Cut tool with your hands. Start from the union, subtract and intersect that three_csg.js already ships; it splits triangles heavily, so three-bvh-csg is the proposal. Cut plane first, then Subtract, Intersect, Union, Split, Erase, Crop. Skins and bones kept. estimate: 4 to 6M units plan, up to 14 to 22M if it overruns like phase 0 did (3.6 times). 1 to 3 h clock.
  3. The rest of the hand brushes. Flatten, Crease, Scrape, Elastic, Buildup, Stamp, a Grab button on the desktop, the Smooth and Indent quick swaps, size by hand, two-hand turn and resize. estimate: 4 to 7M units in total, 1 to 2 h. Basis: each brush is a small change to the built engine.
  4. Masks and the object list. Sculpt only where a bone owns the skin; hide and rename BST objects from the headset. Lock and group already exist in the headset. estimate: 2 to 4M units, 40 to 80 min. Basis: the two row estimates.
  5. Linked copies and kaleidoscope. Evenly spaced copies in a row and snap are already built in the headset. New: copies that stay linked, and the fourth symmetry press. estimate: 2.5 to 4.5M units, 40 to 100 min. Basis: the two row estimates.
  6. Delivery copies with reduction. Reduce a copy to its budget with the master untouched. People are never thinned. estimate: 5 to 8M units, 1 to 3 h. Basis: your plan.
  7. Colour paint. Paint colour on the skin with a fingertip. estimate: 3 to 5M units, 1 to 2 h.
  8. SubD cage and low-poly shapes, OBJ in. The fast simple-shape side from Gravity Sketch. estimate: 18 to 29M units and 5 to 12 h clock. Basis: the sum of the row estimates in the tables, none measured; the biggest unknown.
  9. Clay from nothing. Growing new material in the air. Needs your decision first, because it changes what a figure is made of. estimate: 8 to 14M units, 2 to 4 h.
